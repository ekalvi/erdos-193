# Consequences and related questions for the g85 six-coordinate draft

These are deductions from the draft, not additional external verification of it.
The infinite six-coordinate proof must still be vetted. Literature connections
below distinguish direct consequences from speculative research directions.

## Direct consequences

### 1. Six integer step vectors already suffice in three dimensions

The three-dimensional shadow uses

```
(1,0,5), (0,3,5), (-1,-2,5), (0,-1,1), (1,1,6), (-1,-1,2).
```

The tagged Gaussian proof rules out collinear triples in this walk. Thus the
original small-step construction's sixteen-vector menu is reduced to six.
This is a strengthening of the step-set-size bound in the original geometric
problem, not only an increase of ambient dimension to enforce unit steps.
No global optimality claim about six-element step sets is established here.

### 2. A six-symbol word with no equal adjacent frequency vectors

The six move types form an infinite word. Two consecutive nonempty blocks of
lengths p and q cannot have count vectors C and D satisfying qC=pD. This forbids
weak abelian squares, including unequal-length blocks. In particular it forbids
ordinary abelian squares and all longer consecutive weak abelian powers.

This is stronger than ordinary abelian-square avoidance. Six is not an
improvement on the known four-symbol threshold for the ordinary equal-length
problem. Related background: Sergey Avgustinovich and Svetlana Puzynina,
*Weak abelian periodicity of infinite words*,
https://arxiv.org/abs/1302.4359 and
https://doi.org/10.1007/s00224-015-9629-1 . That paper studies a different global
factorization property; we do not claim to settle all its questions.

### 3. Infinite standard-basis paths for all d >= 6

Appending zero coordinates to the proposed N^6 walk gives one in N^d for every
d >= 6. In the notation of Samuel Korsky, *Long Lattice Paths with No Three
Collinear Vertices*, https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07906, this says L(d)=infinity
for d >= 6. The finite-versus-infinite cases d=4 and d=5 remain unresolved here.
This does not settle those cases or invalidate the methods of the finite-path
paper. Shallit's sixteen-dimensional result already supplies such an infinity
threshold at sixteen; the draft lowers it to six.

### 4. An explicit finite-state sequence for further proof work

Number the eight transitions as

```
a=01, b=12, c=23, d=30, e=02, f=13, g=20, h=31.
```

Their word is generated by the following four-uniform replacement rule, starting
with a:

```
a -> afda    b -> bgab    c -> chbc    d -> decd
e -> afde    f -> bgaf    g -> chbg    h -> dech
```

Code a,b,c,d as A,B,C,D, both e,f as E, and both g,h as F. This yields precisely
the six-direction word in the draft. The finite-state description follows from

```
(sigma(4n), sigma(4n+1), sigma(4n+2), sigma(4n+3))
    = (r, r+1, r-1, r) mod 4, where r=sigma(n).
```

A transition r->s therefore expands to
`(r,r+1), (r+1,r-1), (r-1,r), (r,s)`. This proves the replacement rule, not just
agreement with a sampled prefix. A regression test independently compares its
first 1,023 coded symbols with the binary-digit model.

This supplies a small explicit example for combinatorics on words, finite-state
generators, and formal proof development. It is not by itself an algorithm that
decides all collinearity questions for arbitrary automatic sequences.

## Directions worth investigating, not established consequences

- Prove or refute Shallit's five-symbol substitution; the same normalized-count
  criterion makes the connection exact.
- Search for genuinely different four- or five-symbol sequences. Fixed coding
  of the eight g85/g170 transitions cannot work: explicit finite certificates
  now rule out all such four- and five-symbol codings.
- Adapt finite SAT searches and certificate methods from Aaron Barnoff and
  Curtis Bright, *North–East Lattice Paths Avoiding k Collinear Points via
  Satisfiability*, https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.23226 . Their planar, variable-k
  problem is different; the 6D draft does not solve it.
- Formalize the alternating signed source lemma and the six-vector encoding,
  separately from the existing formalization of the original construction.

No demonstrated consequence for cryptographic security, error-correcting
performance, network routing, or physical collision avoidance is claimed.
The strongest immediate impact is in discrete geometry and word combinatorics.
Also, avoiding equal frequencies in adjacent blocks does not make the frequency
vectors of all separated blocks distinct.

## Explainer and exposure boundary

The interactive source is `design/unit-step-explainer/`. It is deliberately
outside `viz/` and `results/`, the directories copied into the production image.
It has no external runtime dependencies or analytics. Its reading links lead to
public papers, but it makes no background requests to those sites.

The initial preview attempt returned `manifest_missing` for the absent root
`q5m.yaml`. At the user's subsequent request, a development-only declaration
was added, exposing only the generated `design/unit-step-explainer/public/index.html`.
Managed inspect and validate pass. The exact LAN URL and instance lifecycle come
from `q5m-lab dev --json`; no ad hoc server, routing, port allocation, or production
deployment is substituted.
