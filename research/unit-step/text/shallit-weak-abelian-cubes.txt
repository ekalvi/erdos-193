A four-letter infinite word
avoiding weak abelian cubes
Abstract
We give an explicit infinite word over{0, 1, 2, 3} with no factorxyz in which the
three nonempty blocks have the same normalized Parikh vector. The word is obtained
by coding a fixed point of a substitution on five auxiliary letters. The proof uses a
ternary recursion and the quadratic formX 2− 3Y 2: at equal endpoint states, the
3-adic valuation of the form evaluated on a displacement equals the valuation of the
corresponding difference of indices. This excludes four collinear return vertices and
hence excludes every weak abelian cube.
