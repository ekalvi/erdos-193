\section*{Minimum dimension for a no-three-collinear unit-step walk}
Determine the least positive integer $d_*$ admitting an infinite sequence
$P_n\in\mathbb Z^{d_*}$, with $P_0=0$, such that
\[
P_{n+1}-P_n\in\{e_1,\ldots,e_{d_*}\}\qquad(n\ge0).
\]
and
\[
(k-j)(P_j-P_i)\ne(j-i)(P_k-P_j) \qquad(0\le i<j<k).
\]
Here the allowed steps are the positive standard basis vectors, explicitly
\[
e_1=(1,0,\ldots,0),\qquad\ldots,\qquad e_{d_*}=(0,\ldots,0,1).
\]
Each $e_r$ has a $1$ in coordinate $r$ and zeros in every other coordinate.
The coordinate sum of $P_n$ is $n$, so the last condition is equivalent
to noncollinearity.

Prove existence in dimension $d_*$ and impossibility in every smaller dimension.
\begin{itemize}
\item Dimensions 1--3: impossible.
\item Dimensions 4 and 5: unresolved.
\item Dimension 6: proposed construction, awaiting independent review.
\end{itemize}
