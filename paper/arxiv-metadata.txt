Title: A finite-step lattice walk in Z^3 with no three collinear vertices
Authors: Erik Kalviainen
Primary category: math.CO (Combinatorics)
Comments: 9 pages, 1 table, 3 figures. Includes a Lean 4 formalization, independent finite reconstruction, and an exhaustive 500,000-step no-three-in-line check.
Report number: None
Journal reference: None
DOI: None

Abstract:
We give an explicit negative answer to Erdős Problem 193. We define a nested infinite discrete Hilbert path H:N→N^2, select one terminal-steered index from every block of sixteen indices, and lift each selected point by its index. The resulting sequence in Z^3 has successive steps in one fixed finite set and has no three collinear vertices. The obstruction to collinearity is a two-adic pair invariant: for indices with the same terminal quadrant transformation, a valuation of the planar chord equals the ordinary two-adic valuation of the index gap. Applying this identity to the two adjacent chords and their sum rules out a collinear triple. The complete construction has also been formalized in Lean 4 against Mathlib; the formal theorem uses no additional axioms.
